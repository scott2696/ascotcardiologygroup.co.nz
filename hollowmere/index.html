Hollowmere House

You four have inherited a house. Three of you remember a room in it. The fourth is the house itself wearing a guest's face, and it is only pretending to remember.

Exactly 4 players30 minOne phone, or a deck of cards

The four rooms

Never say a room's name or any of its forbidden words out loud.

CardRoomForbidden words
♥The Nursery (something loved)nursery, baby, crib, toy
♦The Library (something kept)library, book, shelf, read
♣The Glasshouse (something grown)garden, glasshouse, plant, flower
♠The Cellar (something buried)cellar, wine, basement, underground

How to play

  1. Turn the lights down and sit in a circle. Whoever holds the phone is Player 1. Player 2 sits on their left, and so on.
  2. Deal secretly. Tap Deal below and pass the phone around so each player sees their card alone.
    • A room: you're a Guest. That's the room you remember, so it is not where the house keeps its heart.
    • The Hollow: you remember nothing and have to fake it.
    • The fourth room is hidden. That's the Heart, the room nobody remembers.
  3. Draw a memory. Tap Draw a memory to get a prompt like "A sound you heard at night".
  4. Tell your memory. Going around the circle, each player tells a short memory of 2–3 sentences answering the prompt. Hide clues to your room in it without using any forbidden words. The Hollow makes one up.
  5. Play 3 rounds. The first speaker moves one seat to the left each round.
  6. The Knock. After rounds 1 and 2, the person holding the phone knocks three times and asks any player one question. The answer has to be one word.
  7. Midnight vote. After round 3, count 3, 2, 1 and everyone points at who they think the Hollow is.
    • If you point at a Guest, the Hollow wins.
    • If you catch the Hollow, it gets a Last Breath and names one room as the Heart. Tap Reveal. If it guessed right, the Hollow still wins. If it guessed wrong, the Guests win.
    • If there's a tie, vote again between only the tied players. If it's still tied, the Hollow wins.
Example memory

You have the Cellar ♠ and the prompt is "A sound you heard at night":

"Dripping. Slow, patient dripping from somewhere below my feet, and the cold coming up through the floorboards like breath."

Tips

Guests: if your clue is too obvious, the Hollow learns which rooms are taken and can work out the Heart. If it's too vague, your friends won't trust you. If someone describes your room, they're probably the Hollow.

Hollow: stay vague, or try to describe the room nobody has mentioned yet.

Playing with real cards instead

  1. Take out the 4 Aces. Shuffle them and put one face-down in the middle without looking. That's the Heart.
  2. Shuffle the other 3 Aces with the Joker and deal one to each player. Whoever gets the Joker is the Hollow.
  3. For memory prompts, draw from the rest of the deck: 2 night sound · 3 smell · 4 something that wasn't yours · 5 the last time you saw the old woman · 6 writing on the wall · 7 never touch · 8 why you stopped sleeping · 9 the weather · 10 something moved · J a door never opened · Q a face in a photo · K a promise to the house.
