Most Likely To

Read a card out loud and everyone points at the person most likely to do it. Then that person has to explain themselves.

4–15 players15 minOne phone

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle where everyone can see each other.
  2. Choose a deck below. Mild is safe for any group and Bold is cheekier.
  3. Tap "Next card" and read the card out loud: "Who is most likely to…"
  4. Count 3, 2, 1, and on "1" everyone points at one person at the same time. You can't point at yourself.
  5. The person with the most fingers has 30 seconds to defend themselves, or to tell the story that proves everyone right.
  6. Pass the phone to the left and the next person reads a card.
  7. Optional scoring: every time you get the most fingers, you get a point. After 15 cards, the player with the most points chooses the next game.
Example

The card says: "…get lost using GPS?" On 3, five fingers point at Ben.

Ben: "That was one time, and the road was closed!" Everyone else: "It was a ferry terminal, Ben."
