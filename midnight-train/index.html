The Midnight Train

You wake up on a train in the dark. Some of you are already dead. You can see everyone else's fate, but not your own, and one of you works for Death.

Exactly 4 players20 minA deck of cards with a Joker

Read this aloud first

"You wake up on a train. It's dark outside the window. You don't remember buying a ticket. A voice on the speaker says: 'Final stop at midnight. The living may leave. The dead stay aboard.' Some of you are already dead. You just haven't noticed yet."

How to play

  1. Forehead cards (alive or dead). Shuffle 3 red and 3 black cards. Deal one face-down to each player and put the other 2 away without looking.
    • Don't look at yours. Hold it against your forehead, facing out, so everyone else can see it.
    • 🔴 Red means alive. ⚫ Black means ghost. There could be 1, 2 or 3 ghosts.
  2. Ticket cards (your secret role). Shuffle 3 number cards and the Joker and deal one to each player. Look at yours secretly.
    • A number card: you're a Passenger. You're honest and you want everyone to find out the truth.
    • The Joker: you're the Conductor, and you work for Death. Trick people into making the wrong choice.
  3. The talking rule. You can never say red, black, card, alive, dead or ghost. You can only describe someone's reflection in the train window.
    • Alive-sounding: "Your breath fogs the glass." "Your cheeks look warm."
    • Ghost-sounding: "I can see the empty seat through you." "Frost forms where you touch the window."
  4. Station 1, "The Window". Each player describes the reflection of the person on their left. Passengers tell the truth, and the Conductor can lie.
  5. Station 2, "The Dining Car Mirror". Each player describes the reflection of the person on their right. Now you've heard about yourself from both neighbours. If they disagree, one of them is the Conductor.
  6. Station 3, "The Tunnel". Turn the lights off. Each player either says one sentence about anyone's reflection, or asks anyone one yes/no question. The person sitting opposite you knows which of your neighbours lied.
  7. Ticket inspection. Count 3, 2, 1 and everyone points at who they think the Conductor is. If the person with the most fingers has the Joker, the Passengers have caught them.
  8. Midnight. Everyone closes their eyes, counts 3, 2, 1, and shows 👍 for "I'm alive, I'm getting off" or 👎 for "I'm a ghost, I'm staying". Then everyone looks at their own forehead card.

Who wins

Passengers win if every Passenger made the right choice. If they caught the Conductor, they're allowed one mistake.

Otherwise, the Conductor wins and Death has new passengers.

Example

Ben is the Conductor. Cara is alive. Ben says, "Cara, I can see the seat behind you through your reflection." That's a lie.

Dan says, "Cara, your breath is fogging the window." Anna, sitting opposite, sees Cara's red card. In the Tunnel she says: "Cara, trust the one who saw your breath."
