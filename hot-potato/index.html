Hot Potato

The phone is a ticking bomb. Say a word that fits the prompt and pass it on, quickly. Nobody knows when it will go off, and the ticking keeps getting faster.

3–15 players10 minOne phone with sound

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle. If you want to keep score, type your names below. Everyone starts with 3 lives.
  2. Choose a type of prompt: Letters, Categories or Rhymes. Then tap Light the fuse.
  3. A prompt appears, like "ING", "Things in a kitchen" or "Rhymes with CAT". The phone starts ticking.
  4. Say a word that fits, out loud, then pass the phone to your left.
    • Letters: a word containing those letters ("ING": swing, kingdom).
    • Categories: something in that category.
    • Rhymes: a word that rhymes.
    • No repeats! If someone challenges your word, the group decides.
    • You can't pass the phone until you've said a word.
  5. BOOM! The timer is hidden and random, somewhere between 10 and 45 seconds. Whoever is holding the phone when it explodes loses a life.
  6. The loser starts the next round. Lose all 3 lives and you're out. The last player standing wins.
Example

The prompt is "ST". Anna: "Stop!" and passes. Ben: "Toast!" and passes. Cara panics: "Er… st… STRAWBERRY!" She passes it to Dan, and it explodes in Dan's hands. 💥 Dan loses a life.
