Defuse

One player has a bomb on their phone. Everyone else has the manual on theirs. The Defuser can't see the manual, and the Experts can't see the bomb. You have five minutes to talk each other through it.

2–6 players5 min a bombOne phone for the bomb, others for the manual

How to play

  1. Pick one Defuser. Everyone else is an Expert.
  2. Experts open the manual on their own phones: ascotcardiologygroup.co.nz/defuse/manual (open it here).
    • With 2 or more Experts, split it up: one takes Wires and The Button, the other takes Symbols and Password.
  3. Sit so the Experts can't see the bomb and the Defuser can't see the manual. Back to back works well.
  4. The Defuser chooses a difficulty and taps "Arm the bomb". The clock starts.
  5. The Defuser describes what they see: wire colours, the button's colour and word, the strange symbols, the serial number.
  6. The Experts find the right rule in the manual and tell the Defuser exactly what to do.
  7. Each part has a light that turns green when it's defused. A mistake gives you a strike and costs 30 seconds. Three strikes and it blows up.
  8. Defuse every part before the clock hits zero. Then swap Defuser and try a harder bomb.
Example

Defuser: "Four wires: red, yellow, blue, yellow. Serial ends in 7!"

Expert: "Four wires… more than one yellow wire, so cut the last yellow one. That's the fourth wire!" Snip. The light goes green.
