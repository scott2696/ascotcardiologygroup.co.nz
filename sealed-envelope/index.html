The Sealed Envelope

Lord Edgar Moreau was found dead in his study at midnight. One of you was in there with him. Find the killer, then open the envelope. It might change everything.

Exactly 4 players25 minOne phone, or a deck of cards

Where you were at midnight

CardRoomWhat it means
♥ ♥The BallroomTwo players were here together, but neither knows who the other one is.
♦The GardenYou were alone, so nobody can back up your story.
♠The StudyYou were with Edgar. You are the killer.

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle. Whoever holds the phone is Player 1. Player 2 sits on their left, and so on.
  2. Deal secretly. Tap Deal below and pass the phone around. Each player sees their room alone. The phone also seals a twist inside the envelope, and nobody sees it.
  3. Round 1: "Where were you?" Each player says which room they were in and gives one detail about it. Innocent players tell the truth. The killer must lie and claim the Ballroom or the Garden.
    • If three people claim the Ballroom, someone is lying.
    • If two people claim the Garden, the real Garden player knows exactly who the killer is.
  4. Tap "Flip a torn letter". It shows one twist that did not happen.
  5. Round 2: "Interrogation". Each player asks one other player one question, and they must answer. Then flip the second torn letter. Now only two twists are still possible.
  6. Round 3: "Last words". Each player gets one sentence to defend themselves or accuse someone. Think about which twist might be in the envelope.
  7. The accusation. Count 3, 2, 1 and everyone points at the person they think is the killer. The person with the most fingers is Accused. If there's a tie, nobody is accused.
  8. Tap "Open the envelope" and read the twist aloud in a dramatic voice. It tells you who wins.

The four possible twists

TwistWho wins
The Plain Truth
The killer did it. No twist.		If the killer (♠) was Accused, the innocent players win. Otherwise the killer wins.
Edgar Is Alive
He faked his death to see who would turn on whom.		The player with the fewest fingers pointing at them wins. That could even be the killer.
The Accomplice
The Garden player let the killer in.		♠ and ♦ are a team and win if neither of them was Accused. Otherwise the two ♥ players win.
The Framing
The two Ballroom players did it and blamed ♠.		The ♥ pair win if ♠ was Accused. If not, ♠ and ♦ win.
Example ending

Anna ♥, Ben ♠, Cara ♥, Dan ♦. Ben lies and claims the Ballroom.

The torn letters rule out "Plain Truth" and "The Accomplice". At the vote, three fingers point at Ben. You open the envelope: The Framing. Anna and Cara were the real killers all along, and their plan worked, so they win.
