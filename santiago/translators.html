Are you a qualified translator?

The practice takes on translators holding postgraduate training and demonstrable experience in legal and institutional documents between Spanish and English. If that is you, write to hello@YOURDOMAIN.com with your CV and two samples of certified work.

Standards

What is required of every translator here, without exception.

The standard does not depend on who signs.

I

Proven qualification

Postgraduate training in translation and demonstrable experience in legal and institutional documents. No one joins on the strength of a self-declared bilingualism.

II

Signed in person

The translator who does the work signs the certificate of accuracy. Their name and qualifications appear on the document you put before the institution.

III

Checked before issue

Names, dates, seals, figures and official titles are verified against the original before anything is signed. No file leaves without that check.

Would you prefer a particular translator to take your file? Say so when you request a quotation and their availability will be confirmed before the work is accepted.
