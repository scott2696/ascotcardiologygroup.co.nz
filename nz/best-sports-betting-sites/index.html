The legal position, in short

The casinos and bookmakers on this site are licensed overseas, not by New Zealand's Department of Internal Affairs. Under the Online Casino Gambling Act 2026 it is unlawful to advertise unlicensed online casino gambling to people in New Zealand, so we publish no links to these sites and no sign-up offers on their behalf. Betting law in New Zealand, in short

Since 28 June 2025, under the Racing Industry Amendment Act 2025, TAB NZ is the only operator allowed to offer online racing and sports betting to people in New Zealand, and offshore bookmakers may not take bets from New Zealanders. TAB NZ runs its betting through a 25-year partnership with Entain, which also operates the online brand Betcha alongside TAB. The offshore sportsbooks named on this site are covered for information only; we do not link to them or carry their offers.

TAB NZ compared with offshore sportsbooks

What to compare between betting sites

Before the 2025 law, comparison sites ranked bookmakers on odds boosts and sign-up bets. For New Zealanders the first question is now legal: only TAB NZ may take your bets. For the offshore brands on this page, the facts that matter are the same as for casinos: who owns the site, which licence it holds, whether it pays in NZD, and how much it lets you withdraw.

Margins in practice

A two-way market priced 1.91 and 1.91 carries a margin of about 4.7%. Priced 1.80 and 1.80, it carries about 11.1%. Over a season of NZ$20 bets, that difference is the single biggest factor in what betting costs you. Our online betting guide shows how to work it out for any market.

Withdrawal limits and win caps

Several offshore books here cap what you can win or withdraw: Bet&Play has a daily win cap of US$50,000; Rivo and Smash cap daily wins at €100,000 and limit withdrawals to €1,000 a day. Combined casino and sportsbook accounts usually share one withdrawal limit across both.

Red flags at any betting site

  • Bets voided after a win for 'palpable error' without a clear explanation.
  • Stake limits imposed after a few wins, then withdrawals delayed.
  • Sign-up bets with rollover on odds of 1.80 or more, which forces many more bets.
  • No licence number, or an owner that is not named.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best sports betting site in NZ?

Legally, TAB NZ (including its partner brand Betcha) is the only online bookmaker allowed to take bets from people in New Zealand. The offshore sportsbooks on this page are listed for information only.

Why can't I bet with offshore bookmakers any more?

The Racing Industry Amendment Act 2025 made TAB NZ the only lawful provider of online racing and sports betting in New Zealand from 28 June 2025. Offshore operators are not permitted to take bets from New Zealanders.

Do offshore sportsbooks accept NZD?

Some list NZD as a balance currency, including Lucky Vibe, Rooster Bet, Fortune Play, Bet&Play and Gunsbet. That does not make them lawful providers in New Zealand.

Which betting site has the best odds?

It depends on the market and changes constantly. Compare margins: total the implied probabilities of every outcome; the lower the total above 100%, the better.

Are betting sites safe?

TAB NZ is regulated in New Zealand. Offshore sportsbooks are only as safe as their overseas licence and their own record, which is what our reviews check.
