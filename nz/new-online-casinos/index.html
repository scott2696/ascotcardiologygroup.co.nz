What counts as a new online casino

New casino brands launch every month, but most are not new businesses. They are new websites from existing operating groups, built on the same platform with a different name, theme and bonus. Several brands on this page share owners and licence numbers: Spinjo and Lucky Vibe both sit under Hollycorn N.V., and Fortune Play and Lucky Circus under Novatrix S.R.L. That is not a bad thing, since an established group usually means working payments and support, but it does mean a 'new' brand inherits its sister sites' complaint history.

How to date a casino by its licence number

Anjouan licence numbers follow the pattern ALSI-YYYYMM…, so the number itself shows roughly when the licence was issued. Rivo's ALSI-202606020-FI1 dates from June 2026; Spino's ALSI-202506006-FI1 from June 2025. A licence date is not a launch date, because brands move between regulators, but it is a checkable fact, unlike the 'launched' dates many review sites print.

Six checks before joining any new casino

  1. Licence number on a real register

    A new brand should show a licence number in its footer that you can find on the regulator's site.

  2. A named operating company

    The terms should say which company runs the site and where it is registered.

  3. Sister sites

    Search the company name. If its other brands have a poor complaint record, expect the same.

  4. Withdrawal limits and win caps

    New brands often launch with low limits. Find them in the terms before you deposit.

  5. Real terms, not just a banner

    Read the full bonus terms: wagering, maximum bet, game weighting and any cap on bonus winnings.

  6. Small first withdrawal

    Withdraw a small amount early to test verification and payout before you deposit more.

New casinos and the 1 December 2026 cut-off

The Online Casino Gambling Act 2026 closed the door to new entrants. Since it came into force on 1 May 2026, no new online casino provider may enter the New Zealand market, and only providers already serving New Zealand before then may continue, until 1 December 2026, unless they apply for a licence. A brand that launched for New Zealand after 1 May 2026 is, on the DIA's guidance, not permitted to be operating here at all. From early 2027 the only genuinely new casinos for New Zealanders will be the licensed ones on the DIA's register. None of the companies named as owners of the casinos on this site (Hollycorn N.V., Novatrix S.R.L., Softon Ltd and Exceptiosoft Ltd) is among them. The DIA has not yet published which brands will run under each licence, but on the public record none of these casinos has a route to a New Zealand licence in this round. Expect them to stop serving New Zealand from 1 December 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the newest online casino in NZ?

Going by licence numbers, Rivo's Anjouan licence (June 2026) is the most recent on our list. Note that under the 2026 Act, no new online casino provider may lawfully enter the New Zealand market until licensing is complete.

Are new online casinos safe?

A new brand has no track record of its own. Check its licence, its owner and the record of its sister sites. A brand from a group with a long, clean history is a lower risk than an anonymous one.

Why do new casinos offer such big bonuses?

To attract players quickly. Large bonuses usually come with high wagering, low maximum bets and capped bonus winnings, so the headline is rarely what it seems.

Will there be new licensed online casinos in New Zealand?

Yes. Eleven companies won the right to apply for the 15 licences at the DIA's October 2026 auction, including SkyCity, TAB New Zealand and Stake New Zealand. Licences are expected in 2027, and licensed brands will be listed on a DIA register.

Can a casino that launched in 2026 serve New Zealand players?

On the DIA's guidance, no new online casino provider may enter the New Zealand market until the licensing regime is in place. Only providers already operating here before 1 May 2026 may continue, and only until 1 December 2026 unless they have applied for a licence.
