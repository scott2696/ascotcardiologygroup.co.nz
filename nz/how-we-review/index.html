What we do, and what we do not do

The Ledger score

Every site gets a score out of 10, built from five inputs. Each input is scored from public information, so anyone can repeat the calculation from the facts in a review.

Score = 0.40 × safety + 0.20 × licence + 0.15 × ownership + 0.15 × terms + 0.10 × banking, rounded to one decimal.

Input 1: independent safety (40%)

We use the Casino Guru Safety Index, a 0 to 10 rating built from a casino's terms and conditions, estimated size, links to related casinos and its complaint record. It is the most widely published independent measure that covers almost every brand here. Where a brand has no profile, it gets a neutral 4.0, so missing information never raises a score.

Input 2: licence (20%)

Input 3: ownership (15%)

Input 4: terms and limits (15%)

Starts at 8.0. We deduct 2.0 for a daily win cap, 3.0 where players report payouts below the stated limits, 1.5 for a weekly withdrawal limit of €2,500 or less, and 1.0 where no withdrawal limits are published. Minimum 1.0.

Input 5: banking for Kiwis (10%)

Starts at 5.0. We add 2.5 for an NZD balance, 1.5 for 25 or more payment options (1.0 for 19 to 24), and 1.0 for crypto support. Maximum 10.

Our sources

  • Operators' own terms, About pages and site footers.
  • Regulators' public licence registers (Anjouan, Tobique, Kahnawake, Curaçao).
  • Casino Guru casino profiles, for the Safety Index, licence verification, owners, limits, payment lists and complaints.
  • Other review sites, only where they add a fact, and marked 'reported'.
  • Department of Internal Affairs, New Zealand legislation and Inland Revenue for law and tax.

Updates and corrections

Every fact is rechecked at least monthly. The current check was completed on 8 October 2026; the next is due by 8 November 2026. When a fact changes, the review is updated and the score recalculated. If you spot an error, tell us; we correct confirmed errors within two working days and note the change.

The order of operator lists on this site is set by our editor and is not a ranking. Where you want our assessment, use the Ledger score, or the table sorted by score.
