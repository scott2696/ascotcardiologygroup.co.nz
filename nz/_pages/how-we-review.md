## What we do, and what we do not do

| We do | We do not |
|---|---|
| Source every fact and show the date we checked it | Link to casinos or bookmakers |
| Say 'reported' or 'not confirmed' when a fact has only one source | Publish sign-up offers, bonus codes or referral links |
| Show independent ratings and attribute them | Claim test deposits, timed withdrawals or game counts we did not do |
| Explain the New Zealand law on every relevant page | Let operators buy a review, a score or a change to one |

## The Ledger score

Every site gets a score out of 10, built from five inputs. Each input is scored from public information, so anyone can repeat the calculation from the facts in a review.

| Input | Weight |
|---|---|
| Safety | 40% |
| Licence | 20% |
| Owner | 15% |
| Terms | 15% |
| Banking for Kiwis | 10% |

Score = 0.40 × safety + 0.20 × licence + 0.15 × ownership + 0.15 × terms + 0.10 × banking, rounded to one decimal.

## Input 1: independent safety (40%)

We use the Casino Guru Safety Index, a 0 to 10 rating built from a casino's terms and conditions, estimated size, links to related casinos and its complaint record. It is the most widely published independent measure that covers almost every brand here. Where a brand has no profile, it gets a neutral 4.0, so missing information never raises a score.

## Input 2: licence (20%)

| Licence situation | Points |
|---|---|
| Kahnawake Gaming Commission | 7.0 |
| Two or more licences (e.g. Anjouan + Tobique) | 6.5 |
| Three licences including Estonia | 7.0 |
| Curaçao Gaming Authority (post-2023 regime) | 6.0 |
| Single Anjouan licence | 5.0 |
| Licence not confirmed or not verified | 2.0 |

## Input 3: ownership (15%)

| Ownership | Points |
|---|---|
| Named owner confirmed by an independent database or the operator's own site | 8.0 |
| Owner named by review sites only (reported) | 6.0 |
| Owner not published or not confirmed | 3.0 |

## Input 4: terms and limits (15%)

Starts at 8.0. We deduct 2.0 for a daily win cap, 3.0 where players report payouts below the stated limits, 1.5 for a weekly withdrawal limit of €2,500 or less, and 1.0 where no withdrawal limits are published. Minimum 1.0.

## Input 5: banking for Kiwis (10%)

Starts at 5.0. We add 2.5 for an NZD balance, 1.5 for 25 or more payment options (1.0 for 19 to 24), and 1.0 for crypto support. Maximum 10.

## Our sources

- Operators' own terms, About pages and site footers.
- Regulators' public licence registers (Anjouan, Tobique, Kahnawake, Curaçao).
- Casino Guru casino profiles, for the Safety Index, licence verification, owners, limits, payment lists and complaints.
- Other review sites, only where they add a fact, and marked 'reported'.
- Department of Internal Affairs, New Zealand legislation and Inland Revenue for law and tax.

## Updates and corrections

Every fact is rechecked at least monthly. The current check was completed on 8 October 2026; the next is due by 8 November 2026. When a fact changes, the review is updated and the score recalculated. If you spot an error, [tell us](https://ascotcardiologygroup.co.nz/nz/contact/#corrections); we correct confirmed errors within two working days and note the change.

The order of operator lists on this site is set by our editor and is not a ranking. Where you want our assessment, use the Ledger score, or the [table sorted by score](https://ascotcardiologygroup.co.nz/nz/casino-reviews/#by-score).
