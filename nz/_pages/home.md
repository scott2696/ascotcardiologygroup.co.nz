### Can an online casino refuse to pay out or hold my winnings?

Yes, within its terms, and that is the most common complaint we see. A casino can delay or refuse a withdrawal if verification is incomplete, if a bonus term was broken (most often the maximum bet while a bonus is active), if the withdrawal exceeds its limits, or if it suspects duplicate accounts or 'irregular play'. Some go further than their terms allow. Your protection depends on the licence: the operator's complaints process first, then the regulator named on the licence. An offshore licence offers far less than a New Zealand one will, which is why this site leads with licence and owner details. Keep screenshots of your balance, bets and every message.

### Can online casinos ban you for winning or close your account?

Offshore casinos usually reserve the right to close accounts at their discretion. A legitimate casino should still pay a balance that was won within the terms. Closures after a big win are often justified by bonus abuse, multiple accounts or 'advantage play' clauses, so read those terms before you take a bonus. Bookmakers restricting consistently winning customers is a separate, long-standing practice, and New Zealand punters on Reddit report it at both offshore books and TAB NZ.

### Can online casinos change the RTP or rig the games?

A casino cannot change how a certified game behaves spin by spin, but it can choose which RTP version of a game to run, and many popular pokies are sold at several settings. The difference between 96% and 91% is about NZ$5 more lost per NZ$100 wagered on average. Check the RTP in the game's information panel and compare it with the studio's published figure. One in five recent Trustpilot complaints we read claimed games were rigged; most describe ordinary losing runs, which high-volatility pokies produce often.

### Why is my withdrawal taking so long, and does the weekend matter?

A withdrawal has three stages: the casino's own pending period, verification, and the payment method. Many casinos approve withdrawals manually and work reduced hours at weekends, and international transfers generally do not settle on weekends or public holidays, so a request made on Friday night can easily take until Tuesday. Verify your account straight after sign-up, withdraw by the method you deposited with, and avoid having an active bonus. See [fast payout casinos](https://ascotcardiologygroup.co.nz/nz/fast-payout-casinos/).

### What is a 'no verification' casino, and should I use one?

'No verification' or 'no KYC' casinos let you play and sometimes withdraw without ID checks, usually with crypto. They are the lightest-regulated end of the market. Licensed casinos must check identity under anti-money-laundering rules, and licensed New Zealand casinos will have to verify that players are 18 or over. A casino that never checks who you are is also one that can more easily refuse a large withdrawal by asking for documents at the last minute.

### My bank is asking about a big win from an overseas casino. What now?

Banks must monitor large or unusual international payments under anti-money-laundering law, so a large deposit from an offshore gambling company can trigger questions.
