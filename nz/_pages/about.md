# About Kiwi Casino Ledger [November 2026] : Who We Are and Why There Are No Casino Links Here

## Who we are

Kiwi Casino Ledger is an independent New Zealand guide to the online casino and betting sites that New Zealanders ask about. We started it because most casino 'review' sites are advertising in disguise: they rank casinos by what they pay the publisher, print payout times nobody measured, and say little about New Zealand law. We wanted a record that a reader could check line by line.

## What makes us different

### No casino links

Nothing on this site sends you to a casino or bookmaker, and we carry no sign-up offers or bonus codes. 

### Every fact sourced

Each review lists its sources and the date we checked them. If we could not confirm something, we say so. 

### A score you can rebuild

The Ledger score uses five published inputs and fixed rules, set out on how we review. 

### The law, stated plainly

Every relevant page explains the 2026 Act, the advertising ban and the 1 December cut-off.  

## Editorial standards

- **Accuracy.** Facts come from operators' own terms, regulators' registers, independent databases and New Zealand government sources. One-source facts are marked 'reported'.
- **Independence.** No operator can pay for a review, a score or a change to either. Scores follow the published formula.
- **No invented testing.** We do not claim deposits, withdrawals or measurements we did not make.
- **Corrections.** Confirmed errors are corrected within two working days.
- **Responsible gambling.** We never present gambling as a way to make money, and every page carries the Gambling Helpline number.

## How the site is funded

Kiwi Casino Ledger is funded by its publisher. Because the site contains no links to casinos or bookmakers, we earn no referral commission when anyone opens an account. The order of operator lists is an editorial choice and is not a ranking; our assessment is the Ledger score.
