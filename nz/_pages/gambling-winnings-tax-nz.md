# Gambling Winnings Tax NZ [November 2026] : Is Gambling Taxed on Casino, Betting and Lotto Wins?

Casual gambling wins are not income in New Zealand, whether from a pokie, a bet or Lotto. The exceptions are professional gambling and gains on crypto. General information only

This is general information, not tax advice. If gambling is a significant part of your income, talk to a tax adviser or Inland Revenue. 

## The general rule: casual winnings are not income

Inland Revenue does not generally treat gambling winnings as taxable income for people who gamble for fun. That covers casino wins, pokie jackpots, betting wins and Lotto prizes. There is no gambling winnings tax to pay, and losses cannot be claimed against other income.

## When winnings can be taxable: professional gambling

The position changes if gambling is a business. Inland Revenue looks at the whole picture: how regularly you gamble, how organised and systematic it is, the scale, and whether you depend on it for a living. Professional poker players and some professional punters fall into this category. If you are in business, winnings are income and expenses, including losses, may be deductible.

## Crypto: the trap most guides miss

Inland Revenue treats crypto-assets as property. If you buy crypto mainly to sell or exchange it, gains on disposal are generally taxable. Using crypto at a casino does not change that: if your Bitcoin rose in value between buying it and spending or selling it, the gain can be taxable even though gambling winnings are not. Keep dated records of every purchase, transfer and sale.

## Interest and investment returns on winnings

Once winnings are in your bank account, any interest they earn, or returns from investing them, are taxed in the normal way.

## Worked examples

| Situation | Usually taxable? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You win NZ$8,000 on a pokie playing for fun | No | Casual gambling winnings are not income |
| You win NZ$1 million on Lotto | No | Lottery prizes are not income; interest earned on them is |
| You bet on rugby every weekend for fun and finish the year up | No | Recreational betting |
| You play poker full-time and it is your main income | Likely yes | Gambling as a business |
| You buy Bitcoin, it rises, you deposit it at a casino | The crypto gain may be | Crypto disposal rules, separate from gambling |

## Duty paid by operators

Some taxes on gambling are paid by operators, not players. Offshore gambling operators have been liable for offshore gambling duty and GST on services to New Zealanders since 2023, and licensed operators will pay duties and levies under the new regime. None of these is deducted from your winnings by law.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you pay tax on casino winnings in NZ?

Not if you gamble for fun. Inland Revenue does not generally treat casual gambling winnings as income. 

### Is Lotto taxed in New Zealand?

No. Lotto prizes are not taxable income, but interest or investment returns earned on the prize are. 

### Do I need to declare gambling winnings to Inland Revenue?

Casual winnings do not need to be declared as income. Professional gamblers must declare gambling income. 

### Can I claim gambling losses on tax?

Only if gambling is a business. Recreational losses cannot be deducted. 

### Is crypto gambling taxed differently?

The winnings are treated the same, but gains on the crypto itself may be taxable under Inland Revenue's crypto-asset rules.
