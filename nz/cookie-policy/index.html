Cookie Policy [November 2026]: The Cookies We Use and How to Control Them

Kiwi Stakes sets no cookies. Here is why there are no affiliate or tracking cookies, what third parties receive, and how to control cookies in your browser.

  1. Home
  2. Cookie Policy

The short version

Kiwi Stakes does not set any cookies. There are no analytics, advertising, affiliate or tracking cookies on this site.

Why there are no affiliate cookies

Many casino sites set affiliate cookies that track you to a casino so the publisher is paid when you sign up. We have no casino links, so there is nothing to track.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which does not set cookies for font requests but does receive your IP address. If we ever add a service that sets cookies, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings: in Chrome under Settings, Privacy and security; in Safari under Settings, Privacy; in Firefox under Settings, Privacy and Security; in Edge under Settings, Cookies and site permissions.
