How a casino bonus works

A deposit bonus adds money to your account that you cannot withdraw straight away. To convert it into cash, you must bet a set multiple of it, the wagering requirement (also called playthrough or rollover). Until then, the bonus and usually any winnings from it are locked. If you withdraw early, you normally lose both.

Wagering, worked out in dollars

The formula is simple: bonus × wagering multiple = amount you must bet. Some casinos apply the multiple to the deposit plus the bonus, which doubles it. Then amount bet × house edge = expected cost.

Read the last column carefully. At 40x on deposit plus bonus, a NZ$100 bonus has an average cost of NZ$320 to clear. That does not mean you will lose it, but on average the bonus costs more than it gives.

The terms that matter more than the headline

Types of casino bonus

  • Welcome package: matched deposits across your first few deposits, often with free spins.
  • Free spins: spins on a named pokie at a fixed stake; winnings usually carry wagering.
  • Reload: a smaller match on later deposits, often weekly.
  • Cashback: a percentage of net losses returned, sometimes as cash, sometimes as bonus money with wagering.
  • High-roller bonus: larger match for large deposits, for example CrownSlots' reported 100% up to €5,000 on €500 or more.
  • No deposit bonus: small amounts or spins without depositing; see no deposit bonuses.

When to say no to a bonus

Playing without a bonus is often the better choice. Decline it if you mainly play table or live games (they rarely count), if you want to withdraw quickly, if you like to bet more than the maximum stake, or if the expected cost in the table above is higher than the bonus itself.

Bonuses and New Zealand rules

From 2027, licensed New Zealand casinos will operate under DIA regulations covering advertising and inducements. Offers from offshore casinos are not covered by those rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good wagering requirement?

Under 30x on the bonus amount only is good for an offshore casino. Trixino's 7x on record is unusually low; 35x to 40x is normal.

What is the biggest casino bonus for NZ players?

By headline, Trixino's 777% up to €7,777 and Gunsbet's 285% up to €7,500 are the largest on our records. The size of the headline matters far less than the wagering.

Can I withdraw a casino bonus?

Only after you complete the wagering requirement within the time limit and follow the other terms. Withdrawing early usually cancels the bonus and its winnings.

Do table games count towards wagering?

Usually very little or not at all. Most casinos count pokies at 100% and table and live games at 0% to 10%.

Why was my bonus winnings cancelled?

Common reasons are breaking the maximum bet rule, playing excluded games, using a non-qualifying deposit method, or missing the time limit. Ask support for the exact term they relied on.

Should I take a welcome bonus?

Only if the expected cost of the wagering is lower than the bonus and the terms suit how you play. Otherwise, play with your own money and withdraw whenever you like.
