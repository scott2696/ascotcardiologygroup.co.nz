Licence and ownership

Casino Guru lists Novatrix S.R.L.

Anjouan (the Anjouan Offshore Finance Authority, in the Comoros) became a common licence for offshore casinos after Curaçao tightened its rules in 2023. Its numbers follow the pattern ALSI-YYYYMM…, so you can read roughly when the licence was issued. It is a light-touch regulator: there is a complaints route, but nothing like the protections of a European or New Zealand licence.

Tobique Gaming Commission licenses online gambling from the Tobique First Nation in New Brunswick, Canada. Like Anjouan, it is an offshore licence; it does not make a site legal in New Zealand.

None of these licences is a New Zealand licence. See NZ online casino law for what that means and what changes on 1 December 2026.

Banking for New Zealand players

Bet&Play lets you hold your balance in New Zealand dollars, so a deposit from a New Zealand bank card or account is not converted. That saves the spread a bank charges on every foreign-currency transaction, which is often 2% to 3% each way.

Published withdrawal limits: €5,000 a week, €20,000 a month. Limits decide how quickly a big win reaches you; a site that caps withdrawals at a few thousand a week can take months to pay out a large jackpot.

Win cap: US$50,000 a day (net win). Any amount above this in a single day is not paid, regardless of how it was won. This is set out in the terms and is easy to miss.

Payment options listed for this brand include Visa, Mastercard, Skrill, Neteller, Paysafecard, MiFinity, MuchBetter, Revolut, bank transfer, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT. Availability varies by country, so check the cashier after sign-up. Our payment methods guide explains fees and speeds for each method from New Zealand.
