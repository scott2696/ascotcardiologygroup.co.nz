Banking for New Zealand players

MadCasino lets you hold your balance in New Zealand dollars, so a deposit from a New Zealand bank card or account is not converted. That saves the spread a bank charges on every foreign-currency transaction, which is often 2% to 3% each way.

Published withdrawal limits: NZ$1,400 a day, NZ$4,500 a week, NZ$18,000 a month. Limits decide how quickly a big win reaches you; a site that caps withdrawals at a few thousand a week can take months to pay out a large jackpot.
