What personal information we collect, why, how we protect it, and how to access or correct it under the Privacy Act 2020.

This policy explains how Kiwi Casino Ledger handles personal information under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and its Information Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us by email: your name, address and what you write.
  • Basic server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used to keep the site secure and working.

We do not run accounts, newsletters, analytics or advertising on this site, and we do not collect payment details.

Third-party services

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy. We do not embed social media, video or advertising scripts.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, act on corrections and complaints, and keep the site secure. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never pass it to gambling operators.

Storage and security

Emails are held in our mail provider's systems, which may be outside New Zealand. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to deal with it, normally no more than two years.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@ascotcardiologygroup.co.nz with the subject 'Privacy request'. We will respond within 20 working days, as the Act requires. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
