Alibi

A crime has been committed. Two suspects have five minutes to invent an alibi together. Then the detectives question them one at a time and catch every answer that doesn't match.

4–12 players15 min a caseOne phone

How to play

  1. Choose two Suspects. Everyone else is a Detective.
  2. Tap "Open the case file" and read the crime out loud to everyone. The Suspects claim they were together the whole time, somewhere else entirely.
  3. The Suspects leave the room to invent their alibi. Tap Start the clock; they get 5 minutes. They should agree on as many details as they can: where, when, what they ate, who paid, what everyone wore…
  4. Bring in Suspect 1. Suspect 2 waits outside, out of earshot. The Detectives read out each question from the phone. Suspect 1 has 10 seconds per answer. Type a quick note of each answer.
  5. Swap. Suspect 1 waits outside and Suspect 2 comes in. Ask exactly the same questions. After each answer, the phone shows Suspect 1's note, and the Detectives tap Match or Crack.
  6. The verdict. 3 or more cracks means the alibi falls apart, and the Detectives win. Fewer than 3, and the Suspects walk free.
  7. Next case: two new Suspects.
Example

Question: "Who paid for dinner?"

Suspect 1: "We split it." Suspect 2: "Um… Ben paid, he owed me." That's a crack! The Detectives lean in…
