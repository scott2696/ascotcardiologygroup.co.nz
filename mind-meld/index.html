Mind Meld

A target is hidden somewhere on a scale between two opposites, like Cold and Hot. One player sees it and gives one clue. Everyone else tries to land on it.

2–12 players20 minOne phoneEveryone on one team

How to play

  1. Choose the first Reader. Everyone else is the guessing team. You all play together and share one score.
  2. The Reader taps "Show the target to the Reader" while everyone else looks away. The target is the bright zone somewhere on the scale. The Reader remembers where it is, then taps Hide.
  3. The Reader gives one clue out loud. It can be any word, thing or person that belongs at that point on the scale.
    • For Cold ↔ Hot with the target near Hot, you might say "a cup of tea". For the very end you might say "the sun".
    • Once the clue is given, the Reader can't add anything else or react.
  4. The team talks it over and drags the slider to where they think the target is.
  5. Tap "Lock in". The target is revealed:
    • Bullseye: 4 points
    • Close: 3 points
    • Near: 2 points
  6. Pass the phone to the left. That person is the next Reader. After 8 rounds, look up your score: 16+ is good, 22+ is great, and 28+ means you're reading each other's minds.
Example

The scale is Useless ↔ Useful. The target is a little past the middle, towards Useful.

The Reader says "a spoon". The team argues: "Spoons are really useful!" "Yes, but not THAT useful." They put the slider at about 70%, and it's a bullseye for 4 points.
