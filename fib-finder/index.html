Fib Finder

Every question has a strange but true answer. Everyone secretly writes a fake one. Then all the answers are shuffled together. Spot the truth, and fool everyone else with your lie.

3–8 players20 minOne phone

How to play

  1. Type everyone's names below and tap Start.
  2. Read the question out loud. It has a blank, like "A group of flamingos is called a ___".
  3. Pass the phone around. Each player secretly types a fake answer that sounds believable. If you happen to type the real answer, the phone will ask you to think of a different one.
  4. The phone shuffles all the fake answers together with the real one.
  5. Pass the phone again. Each player secretly taps the answer they think is true. You can't pick your own.
  6. Reveal! The phone shows who wrote each lie and who fell for it.
    • You find the truth: +2 points
    • Someone picks your lie: +1 point for each player you fooled
  7. Play 6 rounds. The player with the most points is the best liar at the table.
Example

"Scotland's national animal is the ___."

The answers: Highland cow · Golden eagle · Unicorn · Loch Ness monster. Two players pick "Highland cow" (Anna's lie, so Anna gets +2). Ben picks Unicorn. It's true, so Ben gets +2.
