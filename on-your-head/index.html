On Your Head

Hold the phone against your forehead. You can't see the word, but your team can. They describe it, act it out or hum it until you guess.

3–20 players1 minute a turnOne phoneTwo teams

How to play

  1. Split into two teams. With only 3 players, everyone plays together and tries to beat their best score.
  2. Choose a category and a time limit below. Then pick one player from Team A to be the Guesser.
  3. The Guesser taps Start and holds the phone against their forehead with the screen facing out. After a 3-second countdown the first word appears.
  4. Their team gives clues. Describe it, act it out, make sounds, anything at all.
    • You can't say the word or part of it. For "Firefighter", you can't say "fire" or "fighter".
  5. Tap the screen. The Guesser taps the right half (green, Got it) when they guess correctly, or the left half (red, Pass) to skip.
  6. When the time runs out, you'll see the words you got and the ones you passed. Each word you got is worth 1 point for your team.
  7. Switch teams and pick a new Guesser each turn. After 3 turns each, the team with the most points wins.
Example

The category is Animals and the word is Penguin. Your team waddles, flaps their arms and says, "It's a bird that can't fly and lives in the snow!"

You shout "Penguin!" and tap the right half. The next word pops up straight away.
