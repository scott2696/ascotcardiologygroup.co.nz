Word Spy

Everyone gets the same secret word, except for one person: the spy. Take turns giving clues, then find the spy before they work out the word.

3–10 players10 min a roundOne phone

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle. The person holding the phone is Player 1. Player 2 sits on their left, and so on around the circle.
  2. Set the number of players and choose a category below, then tap Deal the cards.
  3. Pass the phone around. On your turn, tap to see your card, remember it, tap Hide and pass the phone to the next player.
    • Everyone except one player sees the secret word.
    • One player sees "You are the spy" and only the category.
  4. Give clues. The phone tells you who starts. Going around the circle, each player says one word or short phrase linked to the secret word.
    • If your clue is too obvious, the spy will work out the word.
    • If it's too vague, the others will think you're the spy.
    • The spy has to bluff and give a clue that sounds right.
  5. Go around twice if there are 3 to 5 of you, or once with more players. Then talk it over for one minute.
  6. Vote. Count 3, 2, 1 and everyone points at the person they think is the spy.
  7. The spy's last chance. If the spy gets the most fingers, they get one guess at the secret word. Then tap Reveal to see who was right.

Who wins

The spy isn't caughtSpy +2
The spy is caught but guesses the wordSpy +1
The spy is caught and guesses wrongEveryone else +1

The first player to reach 5 points wins.

Example

The category is Food and the word is Sushi.

Anna says "rice", Ben says "seaweed", and Cara (the spy) says "dinner" 🤔. Dan says "chopsticks".

Everyone points at Cara. Cara guesses "noodles", which is wrong, so everyone else gets a point.
