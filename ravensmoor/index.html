The Ravensmoor Murder

Two teams. One murder. Seven chapters, each harder and darker than the last. The first team to name the killer, the weapon and the motive wins.

2 teams of 1–645–75 minOne phone per teamPen and paper help

How to play

  1. Split into two teams and sit apart, far enough that you can't hear each other whisper.
  2. Each team opens this page on one phone, types a team name below, and gets a pen and paper ready for notes.
  3. Start together. Count down "3, 2, 1" out loud, and both teams tap Start the race at the same moment. Your clock starts.
  4. Solve each chapter. Read the story and the evidence, work it out as a team, and type your answer. A correct answer unlocks the next chapter.
  5. Shout it out! When you crack a chapter, shout "Chapter 3 cracked!" so the other team feels the pressure.
  6. Stuck? Each chapter has 2 hints, but each hint adds 2 minutes to your time.
  7. Sabotage cards. After chapters 2, 4 and 6 you win a card to play on the other team: freeze them, make them whisper, or add a minute to their time. Tap the card to show it to them.
  8. The final accusation. In chapter 7, name the killer, the weapon and the motive. All three must be right. A wrong accusation costs 5 minutes and locks you out for 60 seconds.
  9. Who wins? Your final time is the clock plus all penalties. When the first team finishes, they show their final time. The other team keeps going, and if they finish with a lower final time, they win.
Example

Team Foxes cracks Chapter 2 first and shouts it. They win a Freeze card and play it straight away. The other team has to put their phone face down for 30 seconds. 😈

But the Foxes used 3 hints along the way, so 6 minutes get added to their time. The race is closer than it looks.
