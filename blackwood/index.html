Blackwood Manor

Wolves are hiding among the guests at Blackwood Manor. Each night they strike. Each day, everyone argues, accuses and votes someone out. The phone is the narrator and speaks out loud, so everyone gets to play.

5–12 players30–45 minOne phone with sound

The roles

Wolf 🐺Knows the other wolves. Each night, the wolves choose someone to kill. They win when there are as many wolves as everyone else.
Seer 👁Each night, checks one person to learn whether they're a wolf.
Doctor ✚Each night, protects one person (themselves included) from the wolves.
GuestNo powers, just your wits. Find the wolves and vote them out.

1 wolf for 5–6 players, 2 wolves for 7–10, and 3 wolves for 11–12.

How to play

  1. Turn the sound up on the phone. The phone narrates the whole game out loud, so nobody has to sit out as the host.
  2. Type everyone's names below, in the order you're sitting, then tap Deal roles.
  3. Pass the phone around. Each person looks at their role alone, taps Hide and passes it on. Keep a straight face.
  4. Put the phone in the middle of the table and tap Begin the night. Everyone closes their eyes and drums their fingers softly on the table, which hides the sound of anyone moving.
  5. Follow the voice. When your role is called, open your eyes, quietly reach for the phone, tap your choice, and close your eyes again.
    • Wolves agree silently, by pointing at each other, and one of them taps the victim.
    • The Seer taps a person and sees whether they're a wolf.
    • The Doctor taps someone to protect.
  6. Morning. The phone tells you who was found dead, if anyone. That player is now a ghost (see below).
  7. Day: discuss and accuse. Start the timer and argue it out. Wolves pretend to be guests. The Seer can drop hints, but if they reveal too much the wolves will come for them.
  8. Vote. Count 3, 2, 1, and everyone alive points at someone. Tap the player with the most fingers. The phone reveals their role.
  9. Repeat night and day until all the wolves are gone (the guests win) or the wolves equal everyone else (the wolves win).

Ghosts keep playing 👻

If you die, stay at the table. You can't talk, but every morning each ghost may say one single word out loud. Choose it carefully. And at night, ghosts may make spooky noises.

Example

Morning: "Cara was found in the library, a candle still burning beside her." Cara the ghost says one word: "Left."

Everyone looks at the person on her left. Ben gets very defensive…
