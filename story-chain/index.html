Story Chain

Make up one story together, two sentences at a time. Each player gets a word they have to use, and every so often a twist turns the story upside down.

3–10 players15 minOne phoneNo winners or losers

How to play

  1. Tap "Start a story". You get a hero, a place and a problem. Read all three out loud.
  2. The first player starts the story with two sentences that introduce the hero, the place and the problem.
  3. Pass the phone to the left and tap "Next turn". The next player gets a word they must use. They carry on the story with two sentences that include that word.
  4. Twist cards: every few turns, a red Twist card appears instead of a word. That player has to make the twist happen in their two sentences.
  5. After 12 turns an Ending card appears. The last player finishes the story in that style.
  6. Vote for the best line of the night. The person who said it chooses the hero for the next story.
Example

Hero: a retired pirate. Place: a supermarket. Problem: the lights won't turn off.

Player 1: "Captain Mags hadn't sailed in forty years, but tonight she was trapped in aisle 7. The lights hummed, and they would not go off."

Player 2 gets the word "banana": "She followed a trail of banana peels to the freezer. Someone had been there before her."
